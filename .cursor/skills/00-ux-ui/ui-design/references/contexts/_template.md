# Context template

Use this to derive a context for any domain not covered here. Answer each line in one or two sentences, then design from the answers.

# Context: <domain>

- **Users and setting:** who they are, where and on what device they work, how often, how much time and expertise they have.
- **What they need most:** the 3 jobs that decide success or failure.
- **Stakes:** what a mistake costs (money, safety, privacy, reputation, time).
- **UI posture:** tone, color mood, type feel, density target, light or dark default.
- **Signature screens and components:** the 5-8 things people in this domain expect to see.
- **Domain rules:** safety, legal, vocabulary, units, formats, permissions, audit needs.
- **Imagery:** what real subject matter should be shown and what to avoid.
- **Common pitfalls:** what designs in this domain usually get wrong.

Then check the result against the house rules in `SKILL.md`: calm density, charts only when meaningful, real imagery.
