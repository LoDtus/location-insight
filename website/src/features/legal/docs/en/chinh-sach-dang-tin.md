This policy applies to every listing to sell or rent real estate on Địa Thế. It is part of the Terms of Use. By posting a listing you agree to this policy.

## 1. Địa Thế's role

Địa Thế is a listing tool. It is not a real estate trading floor or brokerage. It does not broker transactions, verify the legal status of any property, charge commission, or represent any party. Posters and interested people contact each other, check matters themselves and are responsible for their own transactions.

## 2. Who may post

You may post only if you:

- have an account with a verified email address and are at least 18;
- are the owner, a lawful representative, a person authorised by the owner, or an individual or organisation qualified to broker under the law, and have the right to advertise that property;
- provide real contact details through which you can be reached.

By posting, you confirm the above is true. The Operator may ask you to show your right to post where there is a complaint or reasonable doubt.

## 3. Mandatory information

Every listing must include:

- the transaction type (sale or rent) and property type;
- the address and map location;
- the area in square metres;
- the price, stating currency and basis (total price, per month);
- the current legal status, as declared by you;
- the Poster's contact details;
- at least one image that belongs to the property being advertised.

Information must be true when posted and updated when it changes. You may add optional information such as direction, number of floors, furnishing and floor plan.

## 4. Poster's responsibility

You are fully responsible for the content of the listing, including the accuracy of the price, area, address, legal status, images and contact details, and for your right to that content. Địa Thế does not examine legal documents and does not guarantee that a listing is accurate.

The price in a listing is the Poster's asking price. It is not a market price and not a Địa Thế result. Địa Thế's location analysis is not confirmation of a listing's value or legal status.

## 5. Prohibited content

You must not post:

- false or misleading information about price, area, location, legal status or condition of the property;
- listings for property you have no right to advertise, or property that may not lawfully be traded;
- images that do not belong to the property, images copied from other sources without permission, or rendered or illustrative images not clearly labelled as such;
- false contact details, or another person's contact details without permission;
- duplicate listings to gain exposure, or listings that are no longer valid but left up;
- promotion of services unrelated to the property advertised, or fraudulent or malicious links;
- content that is unlawful, abusive, discriminatory or intrudes on the privacy of others.

## 6. Listing life cycle

- A listing is displayed for a period that depends on the plan and is shown when you post. When it expires it is hidden automatically. You can renew it manually. Địa Thế does not renew listings or charge for renewal automatically.
- You can edit, hide or delete a listing at any time. Please take it down as soon as the deal is done or you no longer need it.
- Deleted data is removed from the system as described in the Privacy Policy. Copies made by others outside Địa Thế are beyond Địa Thế's control.

## 7. Review and handling of violations

- Địa Thế has no obligation and makes no promise to check every listing in advance. The Operator may review manually or automatically to a reasonable extent.
- Every listing has a **Report listing** button. Anyone may report a listing they believe is false, in breach, or no longer valid.
- When a report is received or a violation is found, the Operator will endeavour to review it within 5 working days, but does not promise this in every case. Depending on severity, the listing may need correction, be hidden temporarily, or be removed; the account may be restricted or suspended under the Terms of Use.
- The Operator may remove content immediately, without prior notice, for clear violations or on a valid request from a competent authority.
- If your listing is removed or hidden, you may appeal through the Contact Channel with supporting information. The listing is restored if the appeal is well-founded.
- Repeated or wilful violations may lead to account suspension.

## 8. Measurement and contact

Địa Thế may count views and phone-number reveal clicks in aggregate form. Địa Thế does not record the content of calls or messages between Posters and interested people.

## 9. Indemnity

If your listing leads to a claim against the Operator or to loss because you breached this policy, you must cooperate and reimburse as set out under "Indemnity" in the Terms of Use.
