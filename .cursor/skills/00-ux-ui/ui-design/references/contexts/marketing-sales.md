# Context: Marketing and Sales

- **Users and setting:** sales reps (fast, mobile between meetings), sales and marketing managers, marketers running campaigns, founders watching pipeline. Metrics-driven teams under targets.
- **What they need most:** know the next action for every lead, keep the pipeline moving, see what channels and campaigns work, report results.
- **Stakes:** revenue, wasted ad spend, leads lost through slow follow-up.
- **UI posture:** energetic but organized. Brand color can be more expressive here; clear CTAs and status. Moderate density; reps need speed over richness.
- **Signature screens and components:**
  - Pipeline board by stage with deal value totals per stage, plus a list view; stale-deal indicators.
  - Lead and contact record with a pinned next step and owner, an activity timeline, quick-log actions (call, email, note, message).
  - Today view: tasks, follow-ups, meetings; quick actions to call, email or message through the channels the market uses (for example Zalo or WhatsApp).
  - Campaign table with spend, results and cost per result against target; funnel with stage conversion; attribution by source.
  - Content calendar; email and landing-page preview; forecast versus quota.
- **Domain rules:**
  - Every record shows owner, last touch and next action; overdue items surface first.
  - Define metrics where they are shown (conversion, CAC, ROAS) and always state the period and comparison.
  - Distinguish total pipeline from weighted pipeline. Avoid vanity metrics without a decision attached.
  - Respect consent and opt-out for contact data; show unsubscribe state.
  - Landing pages in this domain optimize for one conversion; see `patterns/landing.md`.
- **Imagery:** product in use, people in context, campaign visuals consistent with the brand; real screenshots for SaaS.
- **Pitfalls:** a dashboard of 12 charts, public leaderboards that demotivate, burying the next step, long forms for leads, reports nobody can act on.
