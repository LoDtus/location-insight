This document discloses who operates Địa Thế and how Địa Thế meets its duties under e-commerce law.

## 1. Operator

Địa Thế is operated by an individual, not a company. The Operator's identifying and contact details are published in the operator information section or footer of the website as the law requires, and are provided to competent state authorities on valid request.

Send all notices, complaints and requests through the Contact Channel published in the website footer.

## 2. Type of activity

Depending on which features are open, Địa Thế may fall into one or more of the following under e-commerce law:

- **A website providing services**: lookup, location analysis, paid plans.
- **A platform that lets users post listings**: Users post listings to sell or rent real estate, and Địa Thế provides the place to post and display them.

Địa Thế does **not** provide a real estate trading floor or brokerage service. It does not organise transactions, charge commission, or represent any party.

## 3. Procedures with state authorities

Before offering a feature that must be notified to or registered with the state e-commerce authority, the Operator completes the corresponding procedure under the rules in force at that time. A feature that does not yet meet the legal conditions to operate will not be opened, or will be switched off, until the procedure is complete.

Information on notification or registration, where it exists, is shown on the website.

## 4. Transaction information

Before a transaction Địa Thế discloses the service name, price and tax, payment method, term, and cancellation and refund conditions (see Pricing, Payment and Refunds) and the terms of the transaction (see Terms of Use). You confirm the main conditions again before paying.

## 5. Responsibility for listings and third-party offers

Listings are created by Users and are the responsibility of the Poster. The Operator handles violating content under the Listing Policy and the Operating Procedures: receiving reports, removing or hiding content where justified, cooperating with competent authorities, and keeping handling records. Not checking every listing in advance does not remove the Operator's rights and duties under the law.

## 6. Complaints and dispute resolution

Customers may complain about the service through the Contact Channel. The resolution process is in the Terms of Use and the Operating Procedures. This does not limit your right to complain to a competent state authority.

## 7. Personal information

Collection and processing of personal data is described in the Privacy Policy.
