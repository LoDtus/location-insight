*Version 1.0 · Updated 26 October 2026*

This document describes how the Operator controls content and handles reports, complaints and requests from state authorities. Địa Thế is run by one individual, so the time limits below are reasonable efforts, not absolute promises, except where a time limit is set by law.

## 1. Controls on new listings

- Listings get basic automated checks: required fields, plausible price and area, signs of duplicates, and banned keywords and links.
- A listing with unusual signs may be held for the Operator to review manually before display.
- Criteria: correct property type, complete mandatory information, no content prohibited by the Listing Policy, relevant images.
- A rejected listing comes with a reason so the Poster can fix it.
- These checks are not a guarantee of a listing's accuracy or legal status.

## 2. Receiving and handling reports of violations

1. **Receipt.** Through the Report listing button or the Contact Channel, with a reason and a link. The system records a report number and time.
2. **Triage.**
   - Urgent: suspected fraud, exposed personal data, unlawful content. Handled first, and may be hidden at once.
   - Ordinary: wrong price, incorrect images, expired listings.
3. **Review.** The Operator looks at the listing, the account history and the evidence, and endeavours to respond within 5 working days.
4. **Decision.** Keep as is; require correction; hide or remove the listing; restrict or suspend the account.
5. **Notice.** The reporter and the Poster are told the outcome to the extent needed, where practicable. The reporter's identity is not disclosed to the reported party unless the law requires or the reporter agrees.
6. **Log.**

## 3. Handling appeals

Users can appeal an account suspension or a removed listing through the Contact Channel, giving the listing or account and the reason.

- The Operator acknowledges the appeal and reviews any new evidence.
- If the appeal is well-founded, the Operator restores the listing or account and notifies you.
- If not, the Operator keeps the decision and explains why.
- The decision after review is final at Địa Thế level. You keep your right to complain to a competent state authority or to bring a claim.

## 4. Admin action log

Each admin action, such as deleting or hiding a listing, suspending or restoring an account, or issuing a refund, is recorded: who acted, when, the subject, the reason and the related report number. The log can be accessed only by the Operator, is protected against alteration, and is kept as long as needed to show how matters were handled and to meet legal duties.

## 5. Working with state authorities

- The Operator provides information only on a written or otherwise valid request from a competent authority that states its legal basis.
- Only the scope of data the request requires is provided. The Operator may ask for clarification or take legal advice before replying, within the time the request allows.
- Disclosures are logged. Where the law allows, the affected user is told.
- On a valid request, the Operator removes or blocks violating content within the time required.

## 6. Operational incidents

When the system has an incident that affects users, the Operator proceeds in this order: stop the harm, restore service, post a notice on the website or in the app if it lasts, and learn from it. Incidents involving personal data follow the Data Protection Records. Where necessary, the Operator may suspend a feature or the whole service to protect users.

## 7. Changes to the procedures

The procedures may be adjusted to follow the law and the scale of operation. A new version is published with its update date.
