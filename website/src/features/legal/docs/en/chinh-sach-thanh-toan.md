This document sets out pricing, payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds for Địa Thế's paid plans and services. It is part of the Terms of Use and applies only when Địa Thế offers paid features.

## 1. Plans and prices

- The list of plans, their benefits and prices is published on the website's pricing page. Prices are in Vietnamese dong, include tax where the law requires it, unless the pricing page says otherwise.
- The price and benefits that apply to you are those shown when you pay. Later changes do not affect the period you have already paid for.
- The free plan has usage limits and may exclude some features. Limits are shown in the app.
- Pay-per-use services, if any, are quoted before you confirm.

## 2. Payment

- Payment is made through the SePay gateway by bank transfer or QR code. Địa Thế may add other methods and will announce them on the website.
- A payment is recorded only after the gateway confirms the transaction succeeded. The plan is activated immediately after it is recorded, and its term runs from that moment.
- Transfer the exact amount with the exact transfer description (the order code) Địa Thế provides, within the validity period shown on the order. A wrong amount, wrong description or late transfer may stop the system from recognising the payment automatically. The order may then need manual reconciliation: contact us through the Contact Channel with proof of transfer. If you overpay, the excess is refunded under section 4.
- Only people aged 18 or over, or the legal representative of a person aged 16 to under 18, may buy a plan. By paying you confirm that you are one of these.
- Địa Thế does not store your card number, online banking credentials or transaction authentication codes. Bank transfer fees (if any) are yours to bear.

## 3. Renewal and cancellation

- Plans **do not renew automatically**. When the term ends, you choose whether to renew. Địa Thế does not charge you on its own.
- If Địa Thế later adds auto-renewal, it will apply only if you switch it on, with at least 7 days' notice before the billing date, and easy cancellation.
- You can stop using a plan at any time. Service continues until the paid term ends. The remaining time is not refunded, except in the cases in section 4.

## 4. Refunds

You are entitled to a refund in the cases below. Each order may have only one open request per reason, and the total refunded never exceeds the amount Địa Thế received for that order.

- **Within the first 24 hours**: you ask within 24 hours of a successful payment and, according to the system's records, have not used any benefit of the plan. You receive a full refund of what you paid. The plan ends when the refund is approved.
- **Outage caused by Địa Thế**: a service you paid for is continuously interrupted for more than 48 hours because of a technical fault that is Địa Thế's responsibility. You receive a refund in proportion to the outage time against the plan's total term, or extra time added to your plan if both sides agree. The plan continues. The Operator calculates the exact amount and states it in the outcome notice.
- **Địa Thế ends or changes the service**: Địa Thế discontinues the service, terminates your account through no fault of yours, or makes a change that materially reduces the benefits you paid for. You receive a pro-rata refund for the unused time. The plan ends when the refund is approved.
- **Mistaken or duplicate charge**: an incorrect charge is refunded in full. The plan ends if the mistaken charge is the order that activated it.
- **Overpayment**: if you transfer more than the order price, the excess is refunded. Your plan is not affected.
- **By law**: cases where consumer protection law requires a refund. The refund follows what that law requires.

No refund is given for a plan whose benefits have been used after 24 hours, or when the account is locked for breaching the Terms of Use, unless the law requires otherwise.

**How to ask:** in the app, open the paid order and choose to request a refund; enter the reason and the account holder name, bank and account number to receive the money. If you cannot use the form, send the request through the Contact Channel with your account name, order code and proof of payment. The receiving account should be in your own name; Địa Thế may ask for further verification before transferring.

**Timing:** Địa Thế reviews and replies within 7 working days of receiving a complete request. An approved refund is paid within 15 working days of approval. Working days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. If we decline, we give the reason.

**Abuse:** Địa Thế may decline or reverse a refund and related benefits where there is a reasonable basis to believe a request is fraudulent or abusive, for example giving false information, using a plan's benefits and then asking for a refund, repeating requests to obtain free benefits, or making false complaints to a bank. We will state the reason and you may respond through the Contact Channel.

## 5. Invoices and tax

Listed prices include the taxes the Operator is required by law to pay at the time of the transaction. If you need an e-invoice or a receipt, request it through the Contact Channel within the period the law permits, with the details to be invoiced. The Tax Obligations document gives more detail.

## 6. Payment complaints

If you see a wrong charge, or a service was not activated after payment, contact us through the Contact Channel with proof of payment. Địa Thế checks against the payment gateway's data and replies within 7 working days. This does not limit your right to complain to your bank, the payment gateway or a competent state authority.

## 7. Changes

When prices, plans or these rules change, the new version is published with its update date and applies only to later payments, unless the change favours you.
