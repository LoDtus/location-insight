This document explains how Địa Thế deals with tax and records relating to your payments. It states principles only; the Operator's specific tax calculation follows the tax law at the time revenue arises.

## 1. Who pays tax

Địa Thế is operated by an individual. The Operator is responsible for registering, declaring and paying the taxes arising from Địa Thế's revenue, in the form and by the method that tax law applies to individual businesses at the relevant time.

Địa Thế opens revenue-collecting features only after completing the tax procedures needed to collect that revenue.

## 2. Prices and tax

The listed price is the amount you pay. Where the law requires prices to include tax, the listed price includes it. Địa Thế charges nothing beyond the listed price, other than transfer fees your own bank may apply.

## 3. Invoices and records

- After a successful payment you can see your transaction history in your account.
- You may request an e-invoice or a suitable receipt through the Contact Channel, with the details to be issued and the transaction code, within the period the law permits for such requests. The form of the document is issued by the Operator under the rules that apply to the Operator at the time of issue.
- Please give accurate details. A document already issued may not be adjustable if the details you gave were wrong, unless the law allows adjustment.

## 4. Transaction data

Records and transaction data are kept for the periods set by accounting and tax law. Payment gateways and banks may report transaction information to the tax authority as the law requires. This is a legal obligation outside Địa Thế's control.

## 5. Your own tax

Địa Thế gives no tax advice and is not responsible for your own tax obligations, for example when you post a listing to sell or rent property. Tax on property transactions or rental income is yours. Please consult the tax authority or a tax professional.
