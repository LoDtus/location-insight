This policy explains how Địa Thế uses cookies and similar browser storage, and how you can control them. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Địa Thế also uses similar technologies such as browser local storage, session storage and the web app (PWA) cache. In this policy, "cookies" includes all of them.

## 2. Categories

**Necessary** (always on, no consent needed)
Keep the site working and secure: maintaining your sign-in session, protecting against forged requests, load balancing, remembering your cookie choices, and temporarily storing data so the app can run on a weak connection. If you block these, some features will not work.

**Preference** (consent required)
Remember your choices for convenience, such as language, list view style, and your latest filters. Without them Địa Thế still works; you simply choose again each time.

**Anonymous statistics** (consent required)
Measure visit counts and the most-used pages to improve the product, as aggregated figures. This category runs only after you consent.

Địa Thế does **not** use advertising cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not share cookies with advertisers. If this changes, we will ask for your consent before using them.

## 3. Third-party cookies

Some features embed third-party services, for example Google sign-in, maps and payments. When you use them, the provider may set cookies under its own policy, which Địa Thế does not control. Third-party cookies that are not necessary are loaded only after you consent, where the law requires.

## 4. Your controls

- **On your first visit** you see a cookie notice. You can accept all, keep only necessary cookies, or choose by category. Reject and accept options are equally prominent.
- **Change your mind at any time** through the cookie preferences link in the footer. Withdrawal takes effect from that moment.
- **Through your browser**, you can block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking necessary cookies may stop sign-in and some features working.

## 5. Duration

Session cookies are deleted when you close the browser. Persistent cookies last as long as their purpose requires, usually a few days up to a maximum of 12 months. Your cookie choice is remembered so we do not ask every time, and you are asked again when this policy changes materially or after at most 12 months.

## 6. Changes and contact

New versions of this policy are published on the website with their update date. Send any questions through the Contact Channel published in the website footer.
