*Version 1.0 · Updated 26 October 2026*

This policy explains how Địa Thế collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data, and what rights you have. It is written in line with Vietnamese personal data protection law currently in force. If the English and Vietnamese versions differ, the Vietnamese version prevails.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

The Operator of Địa Thế, an individual, is the controller and processor of your personal data. Địa Thế has no other staff or department with access to personal data. Send any personal data request through the Contact Channel published in the website footer.

## 2. Data we collect

Địa Thế collects only what the service needs.

**Account data**
- Direct registration: display name, email address, password (stored only as a hash), and phone number if you provide one.
- Google sign-in: your name, email address and profile picture, as passed to Địa Thế by Google with your permission. Địa Thế does not receive or store your Google password.

**Data you create while using the service**
- Addresses and map pins you enter for analysis, Analysis Results, and analysis history.
- Saved places and personal notes. Only you can see them unless you choose to share.
- Listings you create: text, images, price, contact phone number and other information shown publicly. What you choose to make public is visible to everyone.
- Content you send in reports, complaints and contact messages.

**Payment data**
- When you buy a plan, the payment partner returns transaction information to Địa Thế, such as amount, time, transfer description, reference code and the sender's account name if provided. Địa Thế does **not** store your card number or online banking credentials.

**Technical data**
- IP address, device and browser type, access time, pages visited, system errors, and cookies described in the Cookie Policy.

**Device location**
- Địa Thế does not access your device location unless you press a "use my location" function and allow it in your browser. The location is then used only for that action and is not stored unless you save a place.

Địa Thế does not seek sensitive personal data such as religion, health or biometrics. Please do not put such information in notes or listings.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

- **Providing the service** (basis: performing our agreement with you): accounts, sign-in, analysis, saved places, displaying listings, handling paid plans.
- **Security and abuse prevention** (basis: legitimate interest, within what the law allows): detecting abnormal access, limiting usage, preventing fake listings.
- **Legal compliance** (basis: legal obligation): keeping records, cooperating with competent authorities, tax duties.
- **Support and complaints**: answering requests and handling reports.
- **Improving the service** with aggregated statistics. Optional statistics cookies run only if you consent.

Where the basis is your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

Địa Thế does **not** sell personal data, does not use personal data for advertising or marketing, and does not build behavioural profiles for commercial purposes. If this changes, we will ask for your consent first.

## 4. Sharing data

Địa Thế shares data only in these cases.

- **Service providers acting for Địa Thế**, under their contracts or terms: storage and network infrastructure, authentication (Google), payment gateway (SePay), address-to-coordinate services, and the language-model service used to write commentary. Địa Thế does not send your name, email or phone number to the language-model service; it sends only location data and calculated indicators.
- **Information you choose to make public**, such as the content of a Listing and the contact number in it.
- **Competent state authorities** on valid request under the law. Địa Thế provides only the scope of data the request requires.
- **A successor** to the service as described under "Discontinuation and transfer" in the Terms of Use, with notice to you.

Some providers may process data outside Vietnam. In that case Địa Thế carries out the procedures and safeguards required by law for transferring personal data abroad.

## 5. Retention

- Account data and your content are kept while your account is active.
- When you delete your account, personal data is removed from live systems within 30 days. Backups are overwritten on a cycle and no later than 90 days after that.
- Technical logs are kept for no more than 12 months unless the law requires longer.
- Transaction records and data needed for tax and accounting duties are kept for the periods the law requires.
- Data needed to resolve an ongoing complaint or dispute may be kept until it is concluded.

## 6. Your rights

Under the law you may:

- be informed about how your personal data is processed;
- consent or refuse, and withdraw consent;
- view, correct or ask for correction of your data;
- ask for a copy of your data;
- ask for deletion or restriction of processing;
- object to processing where the law allows;
- complain, report, or bring a claim;
- claim compensation as provided by law.

You can correct or delete much of your data directly in the app. For anything else, send a request through the Contact Channel, stating the request and the account concerned. Địa Thế may ask you to verify your identity to avoid exposing data to someone not entitled to it. Địa Thế responds within the period set by law. Địa Thế may refuse part of a request where the law allows or requires, for example where data must be kept for a legal obligation, and will give reasons.

## 7. Security

Địa Thế applies technical and organisational measures suited to the scale of the service: encrypted connections, password hashing, access control, limiting what data is collected, backups, and controlled access to admin systems. No system is completely secure. You should also keep your credentials confidential.

If a personal data breach occurs, Địa Thế will act to limit harm and will notify the competent authority and affected users within the time the law requires. The procedure is in the Data Protection Records document.

## 8. Under 18

Địa Thế is for people aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and find that someone under 18 has provided data, tell us through the Contact Channel and it will be deleted.

## 9. Changes to this policy

When the policy changes, the new version is published with its update date. For changes that materially affect your rights or the purposes of processing, Địa Thế notifies you in the app or by email, and asks for consent again where the law requires.

## 10. Contact and complaints

Send questions or personal data requests through the Contact Channel published in the website footer. You also have the right to complain to the competent state authority for personal data protection.
