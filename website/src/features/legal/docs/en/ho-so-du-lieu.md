*Version 1.0 · Updated 26 October 2026*

This document describes how Địa Thế manages personal data protection records: recording consent, handling data subject requests, and responding to breaches. It explains the Privacy Policy further. Địa Thế is run by one individual, so the procedures are kept compact, minimise data, and can be carried out by one person.

## 1. Principles

- **Minimisation**: collect only the data a feature in use really needs.
- **Clear purpose**: each type of data is tied to a published purpose.
- **No sale, no advertising**: Địa Thế does not sell personal data and does not use it for marketing.
- **Provable**: the records below are kept to demonstrate compliance when a competent authority asks.

## 2. Records kept

**Personal data processing impact assessment.** A description of the data collected, purposes, legal bases, recipients, any transfer abroad, risks and mitigations. It is updated when a feature collecting new data is added or an important provider changes, and is given to the competent authority on valid request. It is not published in full because it may contain security detail.

**Consent log.** For each consent that must be recorded (optional cookies, acceptance of terms, separate consents), Địa Thế stores: the account or an anonymous identifier, the time, the text and version of the document shown, and your choice. The log is used to prove consent and to carry out withdrawal.

**Processor register.** The parties that process data on Địa Thế's behalf, the data they receive, purposes, where they process it, and the terms of service that apply.

**Request and incident log.** A record of data subject requests and breaches, how they were handled and when.

## 3. Handling data subject requests

1. **Receipt.** Through the Contact Channel or an in-app function. Địa Thế acknowledges the request.
2. **Verification.** By you signing in or confirming via your registered email. Địa Thế does not ask for identity documents unless necessary.
3. **Action.** Provide, correct, delete, restrict processing or withdraw consent, within the period set by law. If more time is needed or part must be refused, Địa Thế gives reasons in writing.
4. **Record.** The outcome is recorded in the request log.

Deletion requests: data is removed from live systems within 30 days and backups are overwritten no later than 90 days after that. Anything that must be kept for a legal duty (for example payment records) is kept to the minimum necessary and you are told.

## 4. Handling a personal data breach

1. **Detect and contain.** Stop further exposure: rotate keys, revoke access, patch the vulnerability, suspend the affected feature.
2. **Assess.** Determine the type and amount of data, who is affected, the cause and the seriousness.
3. **Notify.** Notify the competent authority within the period the law sets (currently 72 hours from discovery, if the law has not changed), and notify affected users where the incident may significantly affect them. The notice states what happened, what data is involved, what has been done and suggestions for protecting yourself (for example changing your password).
4. **Remediate and review.** Fix the root cause, record the incident, and update security measures.

## 5. Transfers abroad

When a provider processes data outside Vietnam, Địa Thế prepares a transfer impact assessment and completes the procedures the law requires before transfer, and transfers only the data that is necessary.

## 6. Retention of records

Consent records are kept while the account is active and for as long as needed afterwards to demonstrate compliance under the law. Request, incident and impact-assessment records are kept for the periods the law requires.

## 7. Contact

Send any personal data request through the Contact Channel published in the website footer.
