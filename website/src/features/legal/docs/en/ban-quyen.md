This document sets out intellectual property rights in content on Địa Thế, your rights in content you upload, how third-party data is credited, and how to report copyright infringement.

You may view and use them for personal, non-commercial purposes. Without the Operator's written consent you must not copy, redistribute, sell, create derivative works from, or scrape that content in bulk, unless the law allows it.

The name Địa Thế and its identifying marks are used by the Operator. You must not use them in a way that suggests you are Địa Thế or are endorsed by it.

## 2. Content you upload

Content you create or upload (listings, images, descriptions, notes, comments) remains yours or its lawful owner's.

**Your assurances:** you have the lawful right to upload the content and to grant the licence below; the content does not infringe the rights of others (copyright, image rights, privacy); and for images of people, you have the necessary consent.

**Licence you grant to Địa Thế:** a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence to store, back up, process technically (compress images, create thumbnails), display and distribute that content as needed to operate Địa Thế and in the way you chose (for example publicly with a listing). Địa Thế does not use your content for other purposes, such as advertising outside the service, without your consent.

The licence ends when you delete the content or your account, except for what must be kept to meet legal duties or resolve complaints, or what sits in backups until they are overwritten as described in the Privacy Policy.

## 3. Third-party data and content

Địa Thế uses data from third-party sources under each source's licence. The list, licences and attribution requirements are in the Data Sources document.

- Base maps and amenity data come from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Database License (ODbL).
- Sources are credited in the interface where a licence requires it.
- You have no more rights in third-party data than that source's licence gives. Bulk downloading and redistributing raw data from Địa Thế is not allowed without written consent.

## 4. Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe content on Địa Thế infringes your rights, send a notice through the Contact Channel including:

1. your full name and contact details;
2. a description of the work or right infringed, and evidence that you are the owner or authorised;
3. the URL of the content you say infringes;
4. a statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised, and that the information you give is accurate.

**Handling:** the Operator will endeavour to review within 5 working days. If the notice is well-founded, the content is removed or hidden and the Poster is notified where practicable. The Poster may respond with evidence that they may use the content; the Operator will review it and may restore the content. Removal is an operational measure and not a legal finding about who is right.

Anyone who knowingly sends a false notice to remove lawful content may be liable under the law and may lose the ability to submit reports.

Posters who repeatedly infringe copyright may have their accounts suspended.

If you send feedback or suggestions about the product, you agree that the Operator may use them without payment or credit, excluding your personal data.
