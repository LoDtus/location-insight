This document sets out pricing, payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds for Địa Thế's paid plans and services. It is part of the Terms of Use and applies only when Địa Thế offers paid features.

## 1. Plans and prices

- The list of plans, their benefits and prices is published on the website's pricing page. Prices are in Vietnamese dong, include tax where the law requires it, unless the pricing page says otherwise.
- The price and benefits that apply to you are those shown when you pay. Later changes do not affect the period you have already paid for.
- The free plan has usage limits and may exclude some features. Limits are shown in the app.
- Pay-per-use services, if any, are quoted before you confirm.

## 2. Payment

- Payment is through the SePay gateway by bank transfer or QR code. Địa Thế may add other methods and will publish them on the website.
- Payment is recorded only after the gateway confirms that the transaction succeeded. The plan is activated immediately after it is recorded, and the term runs from that moment.
- Please transfer the exact amount with the exact transfer description Địa Thế gives you. A wrong amount or description may prevent the system from recognising the payment. In that case, contact us through the Contact Channel with proof of transfer so it can be handled.
- Địa Thế does not store your card number, online banking login or transaction authentication codes. Any bank transfer fees are yours.

## 3. Renewal and cancellation

- Plans **do not renew automatically**. When the term ends, you choose whether to renew. Địa Thế does not charge you on its own.
- If Địa Thế later adds auto-renewal, it will apply only if you switch it on, with at least 7 days' notice before the billing date, and easy cancellation.
- You can stop using a plan at any time. Service continues until the paid term ends. The remaining time is not refunded, except in the cases in section 4.

## 4. Refunds

Địa Thế refunds in these cases.

- **Within the first 24 hours**: you ask for a refund within 24 hours of a successful payment and have not used any benefit of the plan.
- **Interruption caused by Địa Thế**: the service you paid for is interrupted for more than 48 consecutive hours because of a technical fault for which Địa Thế is responsible. You receive a refund for the interrupted period or an equivalent extension of your term, at your choice.
- **Termination or change by Địa Thế**: Địa Thế discontinues the service, ends your account through no fault of yours, or makes a change that significantly reduces the benefits you paid for. You are refunded the unused period, pro rata.
- **Mistaken or duplicate charge**: refunded in full.
- **As required by law**: cases where consumer protection law requires a refund.

No refund applies once benefits have been used after the first 24 hours, or where the account is suspended for breaching the Terms of Use, unless the law requires otherwise.

**How to request:** send a request through the Contact Channel with your account name, the time and proof of payment. Địa Thế reviews and replies within a reasonable time, normally within 7 working days. Approved refunds are paid to the account that paid or to an account you provide within 15 working days of approval.

## 5. Invoices and tax

Listed prices include the taxes the Operator is required by law to pay at the time of the transaction. If you need an e-invoice or a receipt, request it through the Contact Channel within the period the law permits, with the details to be invoiced. The Tax Obligations document gives more detail.

## 6. Payment complaints

If you see a wrong charge, or a service was not activated after payment, contact us through the Contact Channel with proof of payment. Địa Thế checks against the payment gateway's data and handles it under this section. This does not limit your right to complain to your bank, the payment gateway or a competent state authority.

## 7. Changes

When prices, plans or these rules change, the new version is published with its update date and applies only to later payments, unless the change favours you.
