## 2. Type of activity

Depending on which features are open, Địa Thế may fall into one or more of the following under e-commerce law:

- **A website providing services**: lookup, location analysis, paid plans.
- **A platform that lets users post listings**: Users post listings to sell or rent real estate, and Địa Thế provides the place to post and display them.

Địa Thế does **not** provide a real estate trading floor or brokerage service. It does not organise transactions, charge commission, or represent any party.

## 3. Procedures with state authorities

A feature that does not yet meet the legal conditions to operate will not be opened, or will be switched off, until the procedure is complete.

Information on notification or registration, where it exists, is shown on the website.
